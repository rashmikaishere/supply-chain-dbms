Automatic Rules: database triggers and views

                

                  What is this page? Database rules run automatically on every insert, update, or delete. If anyone tries to approve illegal or deforested goods, the database stops them automatically.
                

              

            

            

               SQLite Triggers, Views & Indexes
              
            

          


          
          

            
            
            

              

                

                  BEFORE UPDATE Trigger
                  trg_validate_batch_compliance_promotion
                

                
Rule 1: Block Approving Deforested Batches

                

                  If anyone tries to set a batch to “Compliant”, this trigger checks all origin farm plots. If any plot had deforestation after 31 Dec 2020, the database aborts the update immediately.
                

              

              

                Target: batches
                
              

            


            
            

              

                

                  BEFORE UPDATE Trigger
                  trg_validate_batch_compliance_promotion
                

                
Rule 2: Block Batches Missing Verified Legal Papers

                

                  Checks that every origin plot has a verified land title and environmental permit. If papers are unverified (like the Pelalawan plot), the database refuses to approve the batch.
                

              

              

                Target: batches
                
              

            


            
            

              

                

                  BEFORE INSERT Trigger
                  trg_validate_plot_polygon_req_insert
                

                
Rule 3: Enforce Polygons on Plots > 4 Hectares

                

                  Under Article 9 of EUDR, any farm plot over 4.0 hectares must be saved with a polygon boundary (multiple GPS vertices), not a single point.
                

              

              

                Target: plots
                
              

            


            
            

              

                

                  BEFORE INSERT Trigger
                  trg_prevent_mixed_non_compliant_parent
                

                
Rule 4: Prevent Blending Blocked Goods

                

                  Companies are not allowed to mix illegal batches into clean product to hide them. This trigger blocks adding any Non-Compliant parent batch into a processing recipe.
                

              

              

                Target: batch_lineage

Why Our Design Is Good: 3rd Normal Form (3NF)

                

                  What is this page? Why we separate data into 18 tables instead of one big spreadsheet. It eliminates duplicate errors and keeps our data clean.
                

              

            

            

               Relational Architecture: 3rd Normal Form (3NF)
              
            

          


          
          

            
            

              

                
                Before: One Big Spreadsheet (Bad Design)
              

              

                If the company name, farmer address, and country were repeated in every batch row, someone updating the address in one row would leave the other rows outdated. That creates Update Anomalies and corrupts legal records.
              

            


            
            

              

                
                After: 3rd Normal Form (Our Design)
              

              

                Each company is saved exactly once in the organizations table. Batches simply reference the company ID. If an address changes, updating one single row updates it everywhere automatically with zero duplicates.
              

            

          


          
          

            
Why each table is separate:

            

              
              

                

                  organizations
                  Master Data
                

                
Why it is separate:

                
Companies have tax numbers (EORI) and addresses. Storing them separately means farmer details are never duplicated across hundreds of harvest batches.

              


              

                

                  plots
                  Land Parcels
                

                
Why it is separate:

                
A piece of land exists permanently regardless of what crops grow each year. Storing plots separately ensures GPS coordinates never get accidentally modified.

              


              

                

                  plot_polygon_points
                  Geospatial
                

                
Why it is separate:

                
A boundary polygon has 3 to 100+ GPS vertices (1:N relationship). Storing vertices in a child table satisfies 1NF by eliminating repeating groups.

              


              

                

                  batches
                  Supply Lots
                

                
Why it is separate:

                
Each batch has its own weight, harvest date, and safety status. It links to products and companies without repeating their descriptions.

              


              

                

                  batch_lineage
                  Traceability
                

                
Why it is separate:

                
Batches can be blended or split (M:N relationship). A dedicated junction table enables recursive SQL queries to trace back 10 steps to the farm.

              


              

                

                  due_diligence_statements
                  EU Clearance
                

                
Why it is separate:

                
One official EU report can contain 10 batches. Separating reports prevents repeating customs reference numbers on every batch row.

              


            

          


        


        
        

          
          

            

              

                
Word Meanings: EUDR glossary in simple words

                

                  What is this page? A plain English guide to all laws, technical terms, and acronyms used in this system.
                

              

            

            

               Plain English Terminology Dictionary
              
            

          


          
          

            
            

              
EUDR

              
European Union Deforestation Regulation

              
The European law (Reg. 2023/1115) that forbids selling goods produced on land cleared after 31 Dec 2020.

            


            

              
Commodity

              
Regulated Product

              
The 7 products covered by law: cocoa, coffee, oil palm, rubber, soya, cattle, and wood.

            


            

              
Plot

              
Farmland Parcel

              
A specific piece of land with recorded GPS coordinates where crops are grown.

            


            

              
Polygon

              
Land Boundary

              
A series of GPS points connected like a fence. Required for all plots larger than 4 hectares.

            


            

              
Batch

              
One Lot of Product

              
A specific quantity of product harvested, processed, or shipped together (e.g. 8,000 kg of cocoa).

            


            

              
Traceability

              
Farm-to-Fork Journey

              
The ability to follow a product step-by-step from the store shelf back to the exact farm plot.

            


            

              
Risk Assessment

              
Safety Check

              
Evaluating country risk, satellite images, and legal papers to make sure goods are clean.

            


            

              
Mitigation

              
Corrective Action

              
Extra steps taken if risk is found, like doing a satellite check or requesting official tax papers.

            


            

              
DDS / EU Report

              
Due Diligence Statement

              
The official compliance statement submitted to the EU customs portal before selling products in the EU.

            


            

              
EORI

              
EU Tax / Customs Number

              
A unique European registration number used by customs to identify trading companies.

            


            

              
Operator

              
EU Importer

              
The company that brings the goods into the EU market and carries legal responsibility.

            


            

              
Cut-Off Date

              
31 December 2020

              
The legal cut-off line. Land cleared of forest after this date is illegal to import from.
