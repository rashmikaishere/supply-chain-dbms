3NF Normalization & Relational Proofs

            
Academic justification of schema design adhering to 1NF, 2NF, and 3NF.

          


          

            
            

              

                1
                
First Normal Form (1NF)

              

              

                All attribute domains are atomic. Polygon coordinates are NOT stored as delimited comma strings; instead, they are normalized into child table plot_polygon_points with sequential vertex coordinates. Every table features an explicit Primary Key.
              

            


            
            

              

                2
                
Second Normal Form (2NF)

              

              

                Every non-prime attribute is fully functionally dependent on the entire primary key. In composite junction tables like batch_plot_sources (batch_id, plot_id), the contribution quantity is fully dependent on both keys, eliminating partial dependencies.
              

            


            
            

              

                3
                
Third Normal Form (3NF)

              

              

                Zero transitive dependencies (X → Y → Z). Country risk levels, commodity descriptions, and operator addresses are partitioned into reference tables (countries, commodities, organizations) rather than duplicated across batches or plots.
              

            


            
            

              

                FK
                
Referential & Domain Integrity

              

              

                Enforces PRAGMA foreign_keys = ON, ON DELETE RESTRICT on critical supply chain nodes, strict CHECK constraints on coordinates, ISO codes, and dates.
